Frequently Asked Questions

Find answers to the most common questions about IronCore Fitness

What are your membership options?

We offer monthly, quarterly, and annual membership plans. Each plan gives you full access to our facility, equipment, and group classes. Personal training sessions can be added separately.

Do I need to be fit to join?

Not at all! We welcome members of all fitness levels. Our trainers will assess your current fitness level and create a personalized program that suits your needs and goals.

What equipment do you have?

We have a complete range of equipment including free weights, cable machines, cardio machines (treadmills, ellipticals, bikes), squat racks, and specialized equipment for functional training.

Do you offer nutrition guidance?

Yes, we offer nutrition consultations with our certified nutritionists. We believe that proper nutrition is essential for achieving your fitness goals.

What are your operating hours?

We are open Monday through Friday from 5:00 AM to 11:00 PM, and Saturday through Sunday from 6:00 AM to 9:00 PM.

Is there a trial period?

Yes! We offer a 7-day free trial for new members. This gives you full access to our facility and classes so you can see if IronCore is the right fit for you.
